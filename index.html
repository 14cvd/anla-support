Anla support

Anla is a free app for learning sign language. It starts with American Sign Language.

Need help or found a wrong sign?

In the app, open a sign and tap “Report a wrong sign”. It opens your mail app with a prepared message. You can also write to abbas3liyev@gmail.com directly.

About the signs

The signs are animated from motion extracted by machine from the PopSign ASL dataset (CC BY 4.0). They have not been reviewed by a sign language teacher, so mistakes are possible. Reports help us fix them.

Privacy

Anla collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
