Anla privacy policy

Applies to the Anla app for iPhone. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Anla is a free app for learning sign language. This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Anla has no account and no sign-in. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that collects data. This version of the app does not connect to any server.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, such as the signs you have learned and your chosen language, is stored on your phone only. We never receive it. If you delete the app, it is gone.

The camera

The practice screen can show your own camera picture on screen, like a mirror, so you can compare your hands with the sign. The app does not record, save or send any picture or video. The camera is asked for only when you open that screen, and you can say no and keep using the rest of the app.

Reporting a wrong sign

If you tap "Report a wrong sign", the app opens your own mail app with a prepared message that names the sign, the pack version and the app language. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you do, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and we use them only to answer you and fix the sign.

Children

Anla collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Where the signs come from

The signs are animated from motion extracted from the PopSign ASL dataset (CC BY 4.0). They were extracted by machine and have not been reviewed by a sign language teacher. Please tell us if a sign looks wrong.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. If the app ever starts to collect anything, the policy will say so before that version is released.
